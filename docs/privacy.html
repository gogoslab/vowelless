Vowelless Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Vowelless is a word-guessing game. Most of the game works entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and who else is involved.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, sell, or share your personal information ourselves. Some features use services from Apple and Google, described below. Each of them is covered by that company's own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Vowelless offers optional video ads: you can choose to watch one to earn coins or double your daily reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized. If you don't consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Vowelless does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Daily puzzles (Google Firebase)

Once a month, the app downloads that month's daily puzzles from a Google Firebase Realtime Database and keeps them on your device. The request doesn't include any account, identifier or game data; like any web request, it reveals your IP address and basic device information to Google, which hosts the puzzles. See Firebase's privacy information.

Online races (Game Center)

Online races use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname is shown to the players you race, and match messages (which clue is live and who answered first) are exchanged through Apple's servers. Vowelless does not receive your Apple Account details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; Vowelless only learns which coin pack was bought, so it can add the coins.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone to say an answer, Vowelless asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device when possible and otherwise by Apple's servers. The text is only used to check your answer; Vowelless does not record or keep your voice.

Your Music library

If you turn on Use my Music library in Settings, Vowelless asks for permission to read your Music library. With your permission, it reads song titles, artist and album names, and genres to create puzzles and hints. This happens only on your device: these names are never uploaded, saved elsewhere, or shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

Data stored on your device

Vowelless saves your game preferences (such as difficulty and music source), your coins, and your daily reward streak on your device. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Vowelless is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
