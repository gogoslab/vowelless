Vowelless Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Vowelless is a word-guessing game. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Data we collect

None. Vowelless does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your Music library

If you turn on Use my Music library in Settings, Vowelless asks for permission to read your Music library. With your permission, it reads song titles and artist names to create puzzles. This happens only on your device: these names are never uploaded, saved elsewhere, or shared with anyone. You can turn access off at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

Data stored on your device

Vowelless saves your game preferences, such as difficulty and music source, on your device. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Vowelless is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
